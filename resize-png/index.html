PNG vs JPG: Which Image Should You Resize?

PNG and JPG are both popular image formats, but they are commonly used for different purposes. PNG is especially useful for graphics, logos, screenshots, illustrations, and images where transparency or sharp edges are important.

JPG is generally more suitable for photographs and images with many colors. If you're working with a logo, icon, screenshot, or transparent graphic, PNG can be a better choice because it is designed to preserve those visual elements.

How to Resize PNG Without Losing Quality

The quality of a resized PNG depends on the original image, its dimensions, and how much you change its size. Starting with a high-resolution PNG generally provides better results when creating a smaller version.

If you need to reduce the size of a PNG graphic, avoid making it unnecessarily small because important details may become difficult to see. For logos, icons, and illustrations, choose dimensions that keep text, lines, and other visual elements clear.

Maintaining the original aspect ratio is also important. Changing the width and height independently can stretch or compress your graphic. Keeping the proportions consistent helps preserve the natural appearance of the original PNG.

Why PNG Transparency Matters

Transparent backgrounds are one of the reasons PNG is widely used for logos, icons, website graphics, and digital designs. Unlike an image with a solid background, a transparent PNG can be placed over different colors or backgrounds without showing an unwanted rectangular area.

When resizing a transparent graphic, it is important to use dimensions that match the space where the graphic will be displayed. This is especially useful for website logos, navigation icons, app graphics, and design elements.
