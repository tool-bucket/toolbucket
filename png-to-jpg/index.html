PNG vs JPG

PNG and JPG are two of the most widely used image formats, but each serves a different purpose. Choosing the right format depends on how you plan to use your image.

PNG

Supports transparent backgrounds and lossless quality, making it ideal for logos, icons, graphics, and screenshots.

JPG

Uses compression to reduce file size while maintaining excellent visual quality, making it perfect for photos and websites.

When to Use PNG

Use PNG when you need transparency or want to preserve every image detail without compression.

When to Use JPG

Choose JPG for photographs, blog images, email attachments, websites, and social media uploads.
