How to Resize JPG Without Losing Quality

Image quality depends on several factors, including the original photograph, dimensions, and the amount of resizing applied. When you resize a JPG, starting with a good-quality original image generally gives you better results.

Avoid making an image dramatically larger than its original dimensions because enlarging a small photograph can make it appear blurry. When creating a smaller version of a JPG, maintaining the original aspect ratio can help preserve the natural appearance of the image.

For the best result, choose dimensions that match the actual purpose of the image. A profile picture does not need the same dimensions as a large website banner, and a product thumbnail does not need to use the full resolution of the original photo.
