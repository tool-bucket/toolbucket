What Is an Image Rotator?

An image rotator is an online tool or software feature that changes the orientation of a digital image. It allows you to turn an image clockwise or counterclockwise so that it appears in the correct direction. Image rotation is different from image resizing or cropping because the main purpose is to change the orientation while keeping the visual content of the image intact.

For example, if you take a photograph with your phone and it appears sideways when opened on another device, you can use an image rotator to correct it. Similarly, scanned documents, screenshots, product photos, and graphics may sometimes need to be rotated before they are uploaded or shared. An online image rotator gives you a convenient way to make these adjustments without needing advanced photo editing software.
