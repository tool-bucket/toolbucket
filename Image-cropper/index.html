Tips for Better Image Cropping

A well-cropped image can dramatically improve its visual impact. Keeping a few simple principles in mind helps create more professional-looking photos.

Keep the main subject near the centre or follow the rule of thirds for a balanced composition.

Remove empty space that doesn't contribute to the overall image.

Choose the correct crop ratio before uploading images to social media platforms.

Avoid cropping too tightly unless you intentionally want a close-up effect.

Preview your cropped image before downloading to ensure everything important remains visible.
