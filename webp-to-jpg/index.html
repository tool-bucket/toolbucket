WEBP vs JPG

WEBP and JPG are both popular image formats, but they are designed for different purposes. WEBP focuses on reducing file size while maintaining image quality, whereas JPG is widely supported across almost every device and application.

WEBP

Smaller file sizes, ideal for websites, modern browsers and faster page loading.

JPG

Best for compatibility, printing, editing software and sharing images.

When to Use WEBP

Perfect for websites where loading speed and storage space matter.

When to Use JPG

Choose JPG for presentations, documents, printers, email and older devices.
